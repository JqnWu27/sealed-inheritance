Who decides when the envelope opens?

Two ways to keep a secret until a condition on Ethereum.

A committee holds key shares

  • t of n people can reconstruct the key at any time.
  • They can agree in private. The chain never learns it happened.
  • Bribing t of them is cheaper than the estate, and leaves no trace.
  • If they vanish, the envelope is lost with them.
  • The heir must trust people she has never met.

Cheating is invisible and free.

A sealed record on the owner's ENS name

  • No key exists. The witness is the finalized chain itself.
  • Opening early needs a finalized history without the heartbeat.
  • Forging that history means signing two conflicting finality votes.
  • Ethereum's own rules slash every validator who signs both.
  • Anyone can run the opener. Nobody has to be trusted or paid.

Cheating is on chain and costs stake.

Ethereum decides when it opens. ENS decides where it lives and who may write about it. Validator stake decides what lying costs.
